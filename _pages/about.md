## About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student in Structural Engineering at the School of Civil Engineering, Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology. I began my doctoral studies in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. [Daming Luo](https://faculty.xauat.edu.cn/luodaming/zh_CN/tzysd/95211/list/index.htm).

My current research focuses on AI-driven diagnosis of concrete durability and structural health monitoring. I am particularly interested in applying artificial intelligence, computer vision, machine learning, and multi-source monitoring data analysis to concrete damage identification, durability assessment, and the safety-oriented maintenance of civil infrastructure.

I received my Master's degree in Civil Engineering from Kunming University of Science and Technology (KUST), where my research focused on structural health monitoring for large-scale bridge infrastructure. Under the guidance of Prof. [Wei Xu](https://orcid.org/0009-0006-5958-9288) and Lecturer [Zhiyi Tang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cgAplYkAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN&oi=sra), I investigated deep learning and machine learning methods for detecting seismic events and anomalous data in multi-sensor monitoring systems.

During my master's studies, I developed a multi-channel information-fusion framework combining time-domain, frequency-domain, and probability-density representations of sensor signals. The framework converts multi-sensor time-series data into image representations and integrates feature-level and decision-level fusion methods for seismic-event detection. It was evaluated using monitoring data collected from a long-span cable-stayed bridge.

Before beginning my doctoral studies, I worked as a Technical Engineer at Sinopec Petroleum Engineering & Construction Co., Ltd. My work involved the design, construction, and technical support of petroleum and petrochemical surface engineering projects. This engineering experience strengthened my interest in translating intelligent diagnostic methods into practical solutions for structural safety assessment and infrastructure maintenance.

My research goal is to develop intelligent diagnostic approaches that integrate engineering mechanisms, artificial intelligence, and multi-source sensing data to support the durability assessment and safe operation of civil infrastructure.

## Research Interests

- AI-driven diagnosis of concrete durability
- Structural health monitoring
- Structural damage identification and condition assessment
- Computer vision and machine learning
- Multi-source monitoring information fusion

## 中文简介

我叫钱广才，现为西安建筑科技大学土木工程学院结构工程专业博士研究生，2026年入学，师从[罗大明教授](https://faculty.xauat.edu.cn/luodaming/zh_CN/tzysd/95211/list/index.htm)。

我目前的研究方向主要包括混凝土耐久性智能诊断与结构健康监测，重点关注人工智能、计算机视觉、机器学习及多源监测数据分析方法在混凝土损伤识别、耐久性评估和土木基础设施安全运维中的应用。

硕士阶段就读于昆明理工大学土木工程专业，主要研究大型桥梁结构健康监测中的地震事件和异常数据智能识别，师从[徐伟教授](https://orcid.org/0009-0006-5958-9288)，并在[唐志毅老师](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cgAplYkAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN&oi=sra)的指导下开展相关研究。

硕士研究期间，我构建了融合时域、频域和概率密度信息的多通道分析框架，将多传感器时间序列数据转换为图像表征，并结合特征融合与决策融合方法开展地震事件识别。相关方法使用某大跨径斜拉桥的实际监测数据进行了验证。

攻读博士学位前，我曾在中石化石油工程建设有限公司担任技术工程师，参与石油及石化地面工程项目的设计、施工与技术支持工作。这段工程实践经历使我更加关注智能诊断方法在结构安全评估和基础设施运维中的实际应用。

我的研究目标是面向土木基础设施耐久性与安全运维需求，发展融合工程机理、人工智能与多源感知数据的智能诊断方法。
